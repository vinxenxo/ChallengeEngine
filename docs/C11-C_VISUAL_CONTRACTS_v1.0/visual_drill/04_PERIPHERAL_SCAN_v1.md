# C11-C.9 — VISUAL DRILL / PERIPHERAL SCAN v1
## Family Visual Contract

**Family:** `VisualDrill / peripheral_scan`  
**Identity:** peripheral awareness / fixation + eccentric stimulus / spatial field

### 1. Purpose
Visualize a mathematically structured peripheral-stimulus field while preserving a clear central fixation reference.

### 2. Mathematical visual language
Use radial coordinates, eccentric rings, polar symmetry and deterministic angular sectors. The visual system may be beautiful but must keep the fixation cue and eccentric stimulus unmistakable.

### 3. Composition
A central fixation landmark remains stable. Peripheral targets occupy defined eccentric bands with sufficient angular and radial separation. Body boundaries are never used as stimulus anchors unless explicitly defined by the drill.

### 4. Motion
Peripheral events may pulse, drift along bounded arcs or appear/disappear with smooth envelopes. No strobe-like onset.

### 5. Palette
Central fixation: neutral high-contrast cue. Peripheral targets: controlled accent colour. Structural geometry remains lower contrast.

### 6. Acceptance
Fixation remains obvious, peripheral stimulus is detectable without becoming a central target, timing is continuous and deterministic, and decorative structure never overwhelms the stimulus.

### 7. Banned
No central animated clutter around fixation, no full-screen flashes, no multiple unrelated peripheral events at once unless the drill explicitly specifies them.
