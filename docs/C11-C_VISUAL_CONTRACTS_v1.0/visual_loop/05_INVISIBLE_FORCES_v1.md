# C11-C.5 — INVISIBLE FORCES v1
## Family Visual Contract

**Family:** `VisualLoop / vector_field`  
**Status:** APPROVED FAMILY SPEC / READY FOR ISOLATED PROTOTYPE  
**Identity:** forces / topology / scientific visualization / hidden structure

### 1. Artistic premise
Make invisible mathematical forces visible. The viewer should feel that lines reveal a physical field that exists independently of the rendering.

### 2. Mathematical grammar
- analytic vector fields;
- streamlines;
- attractor/repulsor topologies;
- magnetic-like dipoles;
- vortical fields;
- seeded streamline placement;
- deterministic line birth/death envelopes.

### 3. Composition
The Body may contain a field-wide structure, but the eye must be able to identify at least one major force centre and one directional flow. Avoid uniform parallel lines across the entire canvas.

### 4. Motion
Motion occurs primarily along field trajectories. Streamlines should appear, evolve, travel and dissolve with continuous envelopes. Field topology itself may breathe slowly. No artificial random jitter.

### 5. Palette
Cool blue-grey/near-white field lines with one controlled hyper-saturated accent, default emerald `#00FF7F`. The accent should travel along or concentrate around selected streamlines.

### 6. Depth
Depth is represented through line weight, opacity, density, velocity and selective accent luminance. The result should resemble an advanced scientific visualization, not a UI grid.

### 7. Banned
No chart axes, legends, fake numerical labels, photographic effects, arbitrary noise, or rainbow field maps.

### 8. Acceptance
PASS requires clear force topology, elegant line flow, restrained density, scientific-premium appearance, deterministic output, mathematical loop closure and strict Body containment.
