# C11-C.4 — LIVING PARTICLES v1
## Family Visual Contract

**Family:** `VisualLoop / particle_flow`  
**Status:** APPROVED FAMILY SPEC / READY FOR ISOLATED PROTOTYPE  
**Identity:** matter / fluid / attractors / intelligence

### 1. Artistic premise
A deterministic particle field that behaves like luminous matter flowing through invisible forces. It must feel physically coherent, not like a random particle screen saver.

### 2. Mathematical grammar
- deterministic particle initial conditions;
- analytic vector fields;
- attractors / repulsors;
- vortices;
- curl-like fields;
- speed-derived luminance;
- deterministic trail decay.

### 3. Composition
Particle density may extend across most of Body, but the dominant flow must have one or more readable regions of concentration and one region of relative quiet.

No individual particle may become the unintended hero unless the family configuration explicitly defines a highlighted node.

### 4. Motion
Particle trajectories are functions of `(seed, particle_id, u)`. Trails decay exponentially or through deterministic finite memory. The field must not depend on traversal order for its final result.

### 5. Palette
Base: violet `#3A0CA3`. Faster trajectories move through hot pink `#F72585` toward white. Colour must be derived from a bounded speed/density mapping, not random per-particle colours.

### 6. Depth
Scale, opacity, trail length and local luminance imply depth. Particle size must remain subordinate to the motion field.

### 7. Banned
No photographic smoke textures, random sparkle emitters, arbitrary particle bursts, frame-dependent random respawn, or bloom strong enough to merge the entire field.

### 8. Acceptance
PASS requires coherent flow, attractive density gradients, physically believable motion, deterministic repeated renders, contained Body output and a loop seam that is invisible at normal viewing speed.
