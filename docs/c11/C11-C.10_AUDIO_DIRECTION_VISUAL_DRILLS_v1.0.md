# C11-C.10 — Visual Drill Audio Direction v1.0

The current global ambient master remains the shared deterministic audio contract. The next production layer should add **movement-supporting ambient music**, not a separate soundtrack per family.

## Direction

- Slow harmonic beds with broad spectral spacing, designed to remain intelligible on small mobile speakers.
- Low transient density; avoid strong kicks, brittle high hats and narrow-band piercing tones.
- Motion-linked swells may follow authored macro phases, but audio must never become a hidden timing cue for a mechanic event.
- No stereo-only information essential to the experience; the mix must survive mono/mobile playback.
- Maintain generous headroom and conservative loudness normalization.
- The CTA phase should have a gentle harmonic resolution rather than an abrupt musical stop.

The family remains a single global ambient master for the Visual Drill corpus; family-specific sound design can be represented as bounded modulation layers in a later C7-compliant authoring step.
