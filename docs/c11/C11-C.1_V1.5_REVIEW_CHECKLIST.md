# C11-C.1 v1.5 Review Checklist

Review at least 5 seeds.

For each seed ask:
- Is the dominant shape different from a circular undulation?
- Is the motion clearly a wave phenomenon rather than a flag-like wobble?
- Does the optical depth read as a mathematical surface/ribbon/field?
- Does the palette visibly vary while staying coherent?
- Does the line scale / hero scale change the visual character?
- Is the loop seamless?
- Does the piece remain unmistakably `Geometric Waves`?

The review target is the actual GIF/MP4, not the shader sophistication.
