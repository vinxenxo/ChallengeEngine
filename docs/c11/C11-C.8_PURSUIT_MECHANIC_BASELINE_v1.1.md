# C11-C.8 — Pursuit Mechanic Baseline v1.1

## Purpose

Current authoring/runtime contract for `visual_drill/pursuit`.

## Movement

- Uniform cubic B-spline control points are authored deterministically.
- Arc-length LUT is generated during authoring.
- Per-frame normalized target samples are authored from the LUT.
- Runtime/presentation consumes the resulting state; it does not solve spline geometry.
- Speed modulation remains bounded and is authored from seed + difficulty policy.

## Cognitive events

- `sizygia_count` is authored and deterministic.
- `sizygia_events[].frame_start` is the timing truth.
- Event count and frame starts are copied into `answer_sheet`.
- Camouflage zones are also authored and exposed through runtime state.

## Presentation

- Complex target is presentation-only.
- Radial DOF is presentation-only.
- Background star field/nebula layers are subordinate to target salience.

## Future extensions

Depth/scale simulation and flanker interference are deliberately future mechanics and require their own authoring answer data and regression contracts.
