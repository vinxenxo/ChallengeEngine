# C11-C.1 — GEOMETRIC WAVES v1.5 ART DIRECTION DELTA

Status: PROTOTYPE ART-DIRECTION ITERATION
Scope: `VisualLoop / geometric` presentation-only.

## Objective

Evolve the family from a repeated circular undulation into a family of distinct mathematical wave phenomena.

## Grammar modes

1. `HARMONIC_MEMBRANE` — projected 3D standing-wave surface.
2. `INTERFERENCE_PLANE` — crossing wave fields and nodal interference.
3. `PARAMETRIC_RIBBON` — folded mathematical ribbon/strip with optical depth.
4. `LATTICE_WAVE` — deforming geometric mesh.
5. `ORBITAL_WAVE` — elliptical/spiral wavefronts rather than a fixed circular blob.

## Seed responsibilities

Seed selection MUST influence at least:
- grammar mode;
- palette family;
- macro scale;
- motion/deformation parameters;
- stroke scale or optical depth.

## Palette families

- cyan/magenta
- electric blue/lilac
- turquoise/orchid
- spectral blue
- warm/cold harmonic

No arbitrary RGB noise. Palette selection remains bounded and family-coherent.

## Composition

Body-only art remains inside the C11-B BodyRegion. Optical depth is simulated by deformation, convergence,
scale and layer alpha; no external 3D assets.

## Seamless loop

All motion remains periodic over 10 seconds / 300 frames at 30 FPS.

## Prototype gate

This iteration passes only when physical renders show multiple macro constructions while retaining the
Geometric Waves identity. The prototype may be rejected aesthetically without changing frozen engine contracts.
