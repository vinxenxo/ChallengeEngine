## 0. Artistic Intent

`Fractal Bloom` is the microscopic universe of the Visual Loop system.

The visual metaphor is:

> **Bioluminescent mycelium / neural synapses made visible through mathematics.**

The viewer should feel that the camera is descending into an apparently infinite living structure rather than watching a decorative fractal filter.

The first prototype is **radial**: a central optical nucleus gives birth to branching filaments that expand and reveal new detail as the mathematical zoom changes.

## 4. Fractal Bloom Morphology

### 4.1 Nucleus

The central region acts as the optical seed of the colony.

It may be denser and brighter than the outer structure, but it must not become a solid white disk.

### 4.2 Filaments

The principal structures are fine curves produced from the orbit-trapped Julia field.

Target hierarchy:

- background filaments: dim violet/indigo;
- active filaments: violet/cyan;
- high-energy nodes: near-white;
- halo: restrained and subordinate.

### 4.3 Branching impression

Actual L-system branches are not required for v1. The fractal iteration itself must supply the branching illusion.

No external tree/organic texture is permitted.

## 5. Depth Model

Exactly three perceptual layers:

**Layer 0 — Deep Roots**

Slowest, dimmest, slightly softened mathematically.

**Layer 1 — Bloom Structure**

Hero filament field; maximum definition and visual identity.

**Layer 2 — Active Synapses**

Sparse high-energy accents and local luminous points.

Depth is produced by scale, opacity, phase offset and optical energy—not by photographic depth textures or true 3D assets.

## 6. Zoom and Temporal Grammar

The canonical loop is `10.0 s / 300 frames`.

Every visible temporal parameter must satisfy:

`P(0)=P(1)`

and primary parameters MUST satisfy derivative continuity at the seam.

The preferred zoom model is periodic exponential scaling, for example:

`Z(u)=exp(A·(1-cos(2πu)) + B·(1-cos(4πu)))`

This creates an analytical open → deepen → return cycle without a seam correction.

The camera does not travel linearly through a bitmap. Fractal coordinates are re-evaluated at every frame so newly revealed mathematical detail is generated by the field itself.
