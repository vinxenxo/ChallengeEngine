# C11-C Editorial + Audio + Loop Contract v2.1.2

## Status

C11-C is a presentation-only artistic layer over frozen C11-B. No art-direction change may reopen C11-B engineering boundaries.

## Current delivery baseline

- Social delivery: **720×1280**, 9:16.
- 30 FPS.
- 18.00 seconds / 540 frames for the current review corpus.
- Logical source composition remains 540×960 and is scaled 4/3 for delivery.
- Effective physical bands: Header 0..192, Body 192..1088, Footer 1088..1280.

The frozen `project.godot` remains unchanged. C11-C launchers temporarily override both viewport and window dimensions through `res://override.cfg` during Movie Maker capture and restore/remove that file afterward. Godot documents `override.cfg` as a project-settings override mechanism, and Movie Maker output resolution follows the effective viewport/window configuration. citeturn402691search0turn402691search12

## Editorial

Header line 1: large, real variation/math parameters.
Header line 2: user-facing hook with deterministic Matrix/airport-board transition to family identity and back.
Decorative header/footer rules remain and inherit the active palette.
Footer: compact generation telemetry only; do not repeat `VISUAL LOOP // FAMILY`.

## Color

Editorial colors derive from the active family palette. `C11CColorBoost.gd` raises saturation/value deterministically for mobile delivery without changing hue identity.

## Audio

Sound ON by default. `-NoSound` and `-Silent` disable generation/muxing. The current sound is deterministic ambient presentation audio: no beep-test motifs, restrained low-frequency content, highly correlated stereo, soft envelopes and slow movement.

## MP4

Exactly one canonical MP4 is retained per successful prototype run. Audio-mux intermediates live only in the OS temp directory and are deleted.

## Review

Canonical Art Direction 2.0 corpus: five deterministic random seeds across all five families = 25 videos. Review generation never cleans prototype staging. `-ResetReviewAssets` only resets the review asset folder.

## Production

Final products live below `artifacts\production\audiovisual\` and are protected from review/cleanup. Replacement requires `-Force` and is performed only after a new candidate has successfully rendered and validated.

## Future duration

Variable short/medium/long duration remains deliberately out of scope. A future independent contract may derive duration from deterministic grammar/geometry/seed properties.
