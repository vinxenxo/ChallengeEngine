# C11-C COLOR VARIATION POLICY v1.9.1

Color is a major production variable, not a cosmetic afterthought.

For every family:
- seed selects a palette family;
- seed also selects a continuous color phase / diversity parameter where the shader supports it;
- strong palette separation is preferred over tiny hue drift;
- the family metaphor remains the constraint.

No arbitrary per-pixel RGB noise.
No color choices that violate family identity.
