# C11-C.6 — TRACKING MECHANIC BASELINE v1.2

## C11-C 2.6.0

Tracking remains a deterministic, frame-addressable smooth-pursuit drill. The runtime generator consumes the authored trajectory parameters and never performs presentation work.

The content seed now expands the Tracking definition during the authoring stage. The seed deterministically selects bounded centre offsets, amplitudes, Lissajous frequency pairs, phase offsets, travel cycles and a motion-speed multiplier. The transform does not consume the cosmetic Visual Drill RNG stream.

The presentation-only `tracking_variant` remains cosmetic and cannot modify mechanic truth.

The emitted trail is still `growing_history`; the renderer may only display positions that have already been emitted by the mechanic.

Safe-zone contract remains within the logical Body 540x672, with a minimum 36 px edge clearance before target radius handling.
