# C11-D to C11-E Full-Family Roadmap — V65

## Strategic target

C11-E is intended eventually to replace C11-C as the factory baseline, carrying forward C11-C's deterministic mechanics and production standards while adding the D editorial/layout system and family music integration. This is a conditional future cutover, not an active release authority. C11-C 2.19.12 remains immutable authority until all family gates, regressions, freeze and separate promotion approval pass.

## C→D cutover conditions

The future D factory must preserve C11-C determinism, media geometry, H.264/audio standards, family mechanics and seed invariants while proving D layout/editorial/music. No baseline replacement until all 101 in-scope items and regression obligations meet recorded acceptance, independent review, immutable freeze, and a separately authorized promotion. `release_authority=NONE` until then.
