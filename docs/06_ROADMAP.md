# Roadmap

## C11 — FREEZE

**Status: CLOSED / FROZEN**

C11 consolidated the social presentation architecture and validated deterministic continuity.

## C11-C — Art Direction

**Status: next active scope**

Purpose: adapt visual composition and styling to reference imagery while preserving the frozen runtime contracts.

Allowed: presentation assets, styling, typography, layout polish inside the established regions, visual emphasis, background/foreground treatment and passive renderer choices.

Not allowed without a new checkpoint: simulation mathematics, structural RNG, canonical content schema, `SimulationResult`, mechanics, timeline truth or test architecture.

## C11-D — Final Export

Production-grade exports, metadata, quality validation and distribution packaging.

## C11-E — Distribution

Platform metadata, batch packaging and final operational workflows.

## Next checkpoint rule

A new checkpoint is created whenever a change crosses the C11 freeze boundary rather than smuggling the change into an existing art-direction task.
