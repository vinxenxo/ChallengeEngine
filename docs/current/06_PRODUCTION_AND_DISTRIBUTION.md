# Production and Distribution — Current

## Default product standard

The default product master is `MASTER_1080`: **1080×1920, 9:16**.

`REVIEW_720` (720×1280) remains the historical C11-C review/evidence profile. It is not invalidated or silently regenerated by the new default.

## Output contract

Current validated encoding uses MP4, H.264, YUV 4:2:0-compatible pixels, progressive scan, constant FPS, a 3-second closed GOP, AAC-LC, 48 kHz stereo and 192 kbps audio.

The declared frame-rate envelope is 24–60 FPS. Ordinary social output is validated at 3–90 seconds. Longform uses a separate non-social profile.

## Resolution architecture

Physical delivery is a profile-driven post-capture transform.

```text
content renderer/source capture
       ↓
   profile encoder
       ├── MIN_540  540×960
       ├── REVIEW_720 720×1280
       └── MASTER_1080 1080×1920
```

Historical Challenges specifically use a native 540×960 source capture. Do not apply the Visual Loop/Drill C11-C viewport override to Challenge runtime.

## C11-C continuity

The frozen 2.16.9 Visual Loop/Drill review corpus remains 720×1280 / 30 FPS evidence with a 540×960 logical social frame. Future consolidated production should consume the central delivery-profile contract instead of maintaining separate resolution logic per content family.

## Artifact policy

Intermediate AVI is temporary unless explicitly retained. Final MP4, manifests, effective definitions and social metadata are stored under `artifacts/`. Historical evidence is not silently replaced.
