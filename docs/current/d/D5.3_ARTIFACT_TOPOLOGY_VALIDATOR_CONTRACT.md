# D5.3 - Artifact Topology Validator Contract

<!-- C11D_D5_3_CONTRACT_V1 -->

## Purpose and authority

D5.3 validates consistency among the D5.0 topology audit, the D5.1 canonical artifact manifest, the D5.2 provenance lineage registry, and the physical files referenced by the manifest. D5.1 remains the authority for logical identity and declared content hashes; D5.2 remains the authority for accepted lineage edges. D5.3 creates no competing registry and does not repair repository state.

The validator reads D5.0, D5.1, and D5.2 outputs and their receipts. It reads referenced source receipts/evidence only for explicit D3/D4 checks. It does not enumerate the repository to discover new governed artifacts.

## Filesystem and identity checks

Every manifest `relative_path` and alternate location is checked for physical existence. `MISSING_PHYSICAL_ARTIFACT` reports missing paths. Where D5.1 declares `content_hash_state: VERIFIED`, current bytes must match `content_sha256`; otherwise a `CONTENT_HASH_MISMATCH` is reported.

D5.1 defers its three own output hashes to avoid circular identity. D5.3 checks the manifest and evidence against the external hashes in the D5.1 receipt. The D5.1 receipt has no external self-hash and remains explicitly recorded as one hash-not-declared path; its existence, PASS/CLOSED fields, and output hash claims are validated. It is not silently treated as a content-hash match.

Artifact IDs are recomputed using D5.1's canonical identity object: `artifact_type`, `artifact_role`, `artifact_version`, `producer_component`, and sorted unique `parent_artifact_ids`; serialized as compact deterministic UTF-8 JSON with sorted keys and hashed with SHA-256. Filesystem paths are excluded. For every one-ID/many-location record, all observed location hashes must agree or `IDENTITY_CONTENT_CONFLICT` is reported.

## Lifecycle and route consistency

D5.0 route classifications are used where available. For D5.1 outputs created after the D5.0 snapshot, D5.0's declared route rules are applied to their relative paths: `artifacts/`, `definitions/`, `tools/`, and `docs/current/d/` are active roots; `docs/history/` and explicitly historical/archive paths are historical; explicit temporary and quarantine components retain those route classes.

An ACTIVE artifact in a historical, temporary, or quarantine route; a HISTORICAL artifact in an active route; or a TEMPORARY/QUARANTINED artifact in an incompatible route produces `TOPOLOGY_LIFECYCLE_CONFLICT`. The nine D5.1 ORPHANED records are expected governance records: each must remain noncanonical, ungoverned, without a valid lineage parent, and without cleanup authority. Their physical presence under the broad active inspection roots does not promote them to ACTIVE.

## Manifest and lineage checks

Every D5.2 node must resolve to exactly one D5.1 logical identity. Every edge source and target must resolve to the manifest. Each edge's evidence artifact ID and declared evidence path must resolve to a manifest node and an existing file. Unknown targets report `UNKNOWN_LINEAGE_TARGET`; unavailable evidence reports `EDGE_EVIDENCE_MISSING`. D5.3 checks the D5.2 DAG validation result and does not rebuild or rewrite the graph.

Directionality follows D5.2: `derived artifact --DERIVED_FROM--> source artifact`. The D3 gate requires distinct raw and delivery master artifacts, master DERIVED_FROM raw, raw VALIDATED_BY the D3.2 receipt, and master VALIDATED_BY the D3.3 receipt.

The D4 gate requires evidence-backed plan-to-request edges, governance evidence linked to its plan, and a D4.9 PASS/CLOSED acceptance edge. D4.8 must remain `BLOCKED` with `RENDERER_POLICY_DISABLED`; no accepted `AUTHORIZED_BY` edge may turn the blocked decision into a grant.

## Governance inventory and negative fixtures

The expected governed inventory is 66 ACTIVE artifacts and 9 ORPHANED artifacts among 75 logical identities and 79 physical locations. The 12,304 global unmanaged candidates remain an external inventory count and are not promoted into D5.2 nodes. No cleanup authority is granted.

Temporary fixtures exercise missing physical files, changed content hashes, same-ID copy conflicts, unknown edge targets, and ACTIVE-in-history conflicts by calling the validator's classification functions. Fixtures are created under an operating-system temporary directory and removed when its context closes. No fixture is created under `artifacts/`.

## Gate and outputs

D5.3 passes only when all 79 locations exist, declared hashes match, all 75 identities recompute, all 11 lineage edges and their evidence resolve, lifecycle and route checks are clean, D3/D4 gates pass, negative fixtures are detected, C11-C frozen identity matches SHA-256 `D85425CF18C5211C8497574F4FC66202D613EACC0EC14CD4E2FEF0B24F634A32`, and runtime authority remains `NONE` with production execution false. D5.3 never moves, copies, renames, deletes, or quarantines repository files.

`d5_3_topology_validation.json`, `d5_3_consistency_matrix.json`, and `d5_3_validation_receipt.json` are deterministic and idempotent. The next checkpoint is **D5.4 - Artifact Lifecycle / Quarantine Rules**.
