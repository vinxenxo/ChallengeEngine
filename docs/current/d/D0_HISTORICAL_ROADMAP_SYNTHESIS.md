The historical roadmap is consolidated into a productization-first D sequence. The important change is ordering: recover and normalize inputs before adding interchangeable assets, richer music or new mechanics.

The preserved project history supports this sequence:

```text
D0 recovery and baseline proof
  ↓
D1 Challenge visual parity
  ↓
D2 asset families/templates
  ↓
D3 Music V5
  ↓
D4 production request/personalization
  ↓
D5 artifacts/provenance
  ↓
D6 seed registry
  ↓
D7 matrix/catalog
  ↓
D8 media/release QA
  ↓
D9 Suite evolution
  ↓
D10 new mechanics
```

This sequencing preserves the project's original deterministic architecture while directly addressing the user's next priorities: Atari-2600-inspired interchangeable art families, richer procedural music, per-video personalization and simultaneous GUI/CLI operation.
