## Hard boundary — enforced by contract and test

The adapter can prepare the envelope, but may not emit renderer-native input, call a renderer, start Godot/FFmpeg, create media or authorize production. The envelope carries `renderer_dispatch_invoked=false`, `renderer_input_emitted=false`, `production_execution=false`, `media_output_created=false`, `D4.8=BLOCKED`, `release_authority=NONE`, and `c11c_source_mutation=false`.

The binding preview is **not** accepted by a renderer. Dispatch requires a separate future approval after a versioned D renderer baseline is reviewed/frozen, D4.8 is explicitly authorized, D9.14 full GUI/end-to-end acceptance passes, D9.16 passes, D9.17 explicitly closes D9, and seed isolation, editorial-only determinism, provenance, AV QA and Catalog reproduction have passed. No dispatch switch/CLI flag is provided in this increment.
