## 1. What this increment actually adds

The add-only D-owned tool `tools/c11d/d9/c11e_e4b_loop_family_stage_contract.py` consumes a canonical D9.9 `PLANNED` result, its D9.10 `PLANNING_ONLY_NOT_RENDERABLE` bridge record, and the existing D-only adapter preparation envelope. It produces a deterministic, self-hashed in-memory family-stage contract. It does not modify `universal_producer.py`, the GUI, CLI, D9.8 model metadata, the D9.10 bridge, the D-only adapter, or any C11-C source.

For every concrete Loop selection it resolves the exact family launcher and grammar, records the visual gameplay seed, binds the approved editorial mapping, describes the approved composition geometry, and specifies a silent C11-C visual-stage invocation contract with `-NoSound`. The argument vector is an inspectable preparation artifact only; it is not executed, and its output root remains a placeholder that must later be replaced by a fresh isolated test root under a separately authorized runner.

## 4. Critical seed/audio boundary

The C11-C family launchers call `C11CSafeAmbient.py` with the visual `$Seed` when their sound path is enabled. The approved D visual-stage contract therefore always uses `-NoSound`; `request.music_seed` is retained as a separate canonical identity but is not passed to the visual process. This prevents accidental coupling of music generation to `request.seed`.

The frozen family registry continues to map: Geometric Waves → `CIRCUIT_PULSE`; Fractal Bloom → `ORGANIC_BLOOM`; Sacred Symmetry → `ORBITAL_RITUAL`; Living Particles → `ORGANIC_BLOOM`; Invisible Forces → `FLOWING_VECTOR`.

**Unresolved A/V integration:** D.1 approves only technical delivery fields and explicitly excludes music selection/licensing, per-family music editorial, effects/synthesis, mixing, ducking, gain/fades, loudness, silence defaults and codec-delay tolerance. It does not yet authorize a `music_seed`-bound call to `C11CSafeAmbient.py`, audio generation or mux. A future D-owned audio adapter must prove independent use of `request.music_seed` without changing C11-C and must obtain the necessary selection/mix policy approvals before any audio emission.
