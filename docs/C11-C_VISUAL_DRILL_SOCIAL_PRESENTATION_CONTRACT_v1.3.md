## C11-C 2.5.0 duration policy

The shared 3-second preparation countdown remains presentation-only. Total Visual Drill presentation duration is constrained to 20–30 seconds. Tracking currently uses 21 seconds of gameplay plus 3 seconds of pre-roll; the other canonical drills use 17 seconds of gameplay plus 3 seconds of pre-roll.
